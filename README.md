## About me

I am a product-focused **Software Engineer** who enjoys working across the stack: shaping an interface, designing the data model behind it, enforcing authorization correctly, and debugging the system until the entire experience feels dependable.

- 📱 Built **[Offixia](https://github.com/ExpC0/offixia)**, a multi-organization office platform with attendance, tasks, leave workflows, messaging, granular permissions, and WebRTC calls.
- 🌐 Built **[Almanac](https://github.com/ExpC0/almanac)**, a secure full-stack daily dashboard with custom authentication, PostgreSQL-backed sessions, cross-account authorization, and automated browser tests.
- ⚙️ Professional experience as a **Software Engineer (R&D)** developing networking software for industrial switches and routers on VxWorks and embedded Linux.
- 🎓 B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from **Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET)**.
- 🧩 Solved **500+ competitive-programming problems**, strengthening my data structures, algorithms, and problem-solving fundamentals.
